Chegatta vs TSheets vs BuddyPunch vs Clockify 2026

Detailed comparison of Chegatta against major attendance tracking competitors to help you choose the right system for your business.

On this page
  1. Chegatta Overview
  2. TSheets Overview
  3. BuddyPunch Overview

  5. Head-to-Head Comparison
  6. Decision Guide by Business Type

Chegatta

  • Best for: Multi-agency staffing companies, construction, healthcare, fixed worksites
  • Starting price: $2-15 per employee per month
  • Key differentiators: Multi-company management, Portuguese/US/EU compliance, device fingerprinting, GDPR-ready
  • GPS/Geofencing: Full support with configurable boundaries
  • Buddy Punching Prevention: GPS + geofencing + device fingerprinting + optional biometrics
  • Payroll Integration: Gusto, ADP, Xero, QuickBooks, SEPA (EU)

TSheets (now QuickBooks Time)

  • Best for: Small to medium businesses, field service, construction
  • Starting price: ~$8 per user per month
  • Key features: GPS tracking, geofencing, scheduling, job costing
  • Buddy Punching Prevention: GPS + photo capture, optional QR code check-in
  • Payroll Integration: Native QuickBooks integration, Gusto, Xero
  • Limitations: No multi-company agency support, GDPR compliance less emphasized

BuddyPunch

  • Best for: Small businesses, restaurants, retail, simple time tracking
  • Starting price: ~$4 per user per month
  • Key features: Basic GPS, geofencing, employee database
  • Buddy Punching Prevention: GPS location, geofence, basic device identification
  • Payroll Integration: QuickBooks, Gusto, ADP
  • Limitations: Limited compliance features, no multi-company support, basic reporting

Clockify

  • Best for: Freelancers, small teams, budget-conscious organizations
  • Starting price: Free for basic, $5-10 per user per month for premium
  • Key features: Basic time tracking, reporting, simple GPS (higher tiers)
  • Buddy Punching Prevention: Limited GPS available on premium plans
  • Payroll Integration: Basic exports, no deep payroll integration
  • Limitations: No geofencing on lower tiers, minimal compliance features, basic mobile app

Decision Guide by Business Type

Vendor Choose it if:
Chegatta
  • You run a staffing agency with multiple client companies
  • You need US/EU/Portugal labor law compliance
  • You have remote/field workers needing GPS/geofencing
  • You want to prevent buddy punching with multi-layer verification
  • You need payroll integration with Gusto, ADP, Xero, or SEPA
TSheets
  • You're a small to medium business with field service workers
  • You primarily use QuickBooks for payroll
  • You need scheduling + time tracking combined
  • Budget is $8+ per user per month
BuddyPunch
  • You're a small business with simple time tracking needs
  • Budget is $4 per user per month
  • You need basic GPS and geofencing
  • You don't need multi-company or advanced compliance
Clockify
  • You're budget-conscious (free tier available)
  • You have freelancers or very small teams
  • Basic time tracking is sufficient
  • You can upgrade later for GPS/geofencing
