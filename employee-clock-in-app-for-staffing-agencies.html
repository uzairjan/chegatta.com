Clock-In App

Employee clock-in app for staffing agencies

An employee clock-in app for staffing agencies lets temporary workers clock in from any phone browser while the agency gets verified, real-time attendance across every client company. Chegatta verifies each clock-in with GPS geofencing, tracks breaks and overtime automatically, and needs no app installation.

Why You Need It

A clock-in app is only useful if workers actually use it

The best clock-in app is the one a temporary worker can open in seconds and the one your operations team can trust. Browser-based clock-in removes installation friction, GPS verification removes guesswork, and automatic break and overtime tracking removes the late-night arithmetic.

Clock in without installing an app

Workers open a URL in their phone browser and clock in. No app store, no account creation, no forgotten passwords at the client gate.

Verified, not just reported

GPS clock-in is checked against the geofence of each client's worksite, so off-site clock-ins are flagged and hours are provably worked.

Breaks and overtime on autopilot

Break time is detected automatically and overtime is calculated against each client's shift rules. No manual timesheet re-keying.

Many clients, one dashboard

See verified attendance, lateness, and overtime across every client company at once, so problem sites are visible before your client complains.

Problems flagged early

AI attention scores surface recurring lateness, missing clock-outs, and off-site clock-ins so you can fix issues before they escalate.

Works in your workers' language

Employees clock in in English, Spanish, or Portuguese. No training manuals needed — a temporary worker from any market can clock in immediately.

How It Works

From signup to verified hours in one afternoon

1

Set up your agency and clients

Create your staffing agency, add each client company, and drop a geofence on every worksite where your employees operate.

2

Assign workers to assignments

Invite temporary employees by email, assign them to one or more client companies, and set shift rules for breaks and overtime.

3

Workers clock in, you watch it live

Workers open the clock-in link on their phone at any client location. You see verified attendance across all companies, with anomalies flagged automatically.
