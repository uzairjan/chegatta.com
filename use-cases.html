Use Cases

Attendance tracking built for business clients

When your employees work across multiple work sites, sites, and schedules, spreadsheets and paper time cards stop working. Chegatta gives you one system to track them all.

Who It's For

Built for the sectors business clients serve

Security staffing

Verify guards are actually on-site with geofenced clock-in at every client location, and flag shifts left early or started late in real time.

Hospitality & Events

Rotate temporary staff between venues, track breaks and overtime automatically, and compare attendance across every event your business staffs.

Logistics & Warehousing

Track shift starts across distribution centers, detect missed clock-outs that break payroll accuracy, and keep temps moving between sites.

Facilities & Cleaning

Confirm cleaners clock in at the right building, catch recurring lateness with attention scores, and bill clients with accurate shift data.

Retail & FMCG

Staff multiple stores with the same pool of workers, manage weekend and holiday rotations, and see attendance by store at a glance.

Healthcare & Care

Track nurses and carers across care homes and home-visit routes, with overtime control that protects both clients and workers.

How It Works

From signup to full visibility in one afternoon

1

Set Up Your Agency

Create your business client, add each work site, and configure geofence locations for every worksite your employees operate in.

2

Assign Employees to Clients

Invite workers by email, assign them to one or more work sites, and set up shifts. Roles and permissions are handled automatically.

3

Track Across Every Site

Employees clock in from their phone at any client location. You get one real-time dashboard across all companies, with anomaly detection and insights.

Why Agencies Choose Chegatta

Everything your business client needs in one place

Verify Real Clock-Ins

Geolocation and geofencing eliminate buddy punching and off-site clock-ins. You know every hour claimed was actually worked at the right place.

Compare Clients at a Glance

See attendance, lateness, and overtime across every work site from one dashboard. Spot problem sites before your clients do.

Protect Payroll Accuracy

Automatic break and overtime tracking means payroll is based on verified hours, not estimates. Export clean reports for your accounts team.
