Every industry below shares the same core challenge: verifying that people were where they said, when they said — without drowning admin teams in timesheets.

Security Staffing

Attendance tracking for security staffing agencies

Guards work alone at client sites, often overnight. Without verification, you're paying for hours nobody can confirm — and a single falsified shift at one site can burn a client relationship worth months of revenue.

How Chegatta solves it

  • Geofenced clock-in at every client site — each guard posts' coordinates are checked against the site's GPS fence (default 200 m radius) at clock-in and clock-out.
  • Device fingerprinting — SHA-256 device identity catches a guard clocking in a colleague who hasn't arrived yet.
  • Late-start and early-leave flags — shifts that start late or end early against the scheduled rota appear on the attention list in real time.
  • Client visibility — give each client their own login so they see their site's coverage without emailing you for reports.
  • Offline resilience — basements and car parks with no signal still capture clock-ins; they sync when the phone reconnects.

Verified coverage evidence for every client site, disputes settled with data, and payroll built from confirmed hours instead of phone-call trust.

Hospitality & Events

Temporary staff tracking for hospitality and events

Waiters, kitchen porters and event staff rotate between venues — sometimes several in one week. Paper sign-in sheets at each venue get lost, breaks go unrecorded, and reconciling who worked where at the end of the month eats entire days.

How Chegatta solves it

  • One worker, many venues — assign the same person to multiple sites with an immutable assignment history, so you always know who worked where and when.
  • Automatic break and overtime capture — pause/resume from the phone; overtime is calculated per site rules without manual maths.
  • Late clock-in handling — when someone clocks in after their expected start plus grace period, managers are notified immediately with an optional note from the worker.
  • Per-venue attendance comparison — spot which events consistently run late or understaffed from one dashboard.

Reconciliation drops from days to minutes, and clients receive accurate, itemised hours for every event you staff.

Logistics & Warehousing

GPS attendance for logistics and warehousing teams

Distribution centres, yards, and delivery hubs run around the clock. Agency workers flow in and out across multiple buildings, missed clock-outs destroy payroll accuracy, and night-shift supervisors have no easy way to confirm headcount at 3 AM.

How Chegatta solves it

  • Per-building geofences — separate geofence for the warehouse, the yard, and the office, so clock-ins are matched to the right building even within one campus.
  • Missing clock-out detection — every shift without a clock-out is automatically flagged; admins correct the time and the audit log records who changed what.
  • Rule-based attendance flags — off-site clock-ins, excessive overtime, and long breaks are detected automatically against each site's configuration.
  • Kiosk mode for shared devices — mount a tablet at the loading bay; workers clock in with PIN or QR scan, no personal phone required.

Payroll-accurate hours across every building, with supervisors verifying headcount from their phone instead of walking the floor.

Retail & FMCG

Multi-store attendance for retail and FMCG businesses

One pool of workers staffs many stores. Weekend rotations, holiday cover, and store-level overtime rules differ per location — and head office has no single view of who worked where until payroll day.

How Chegatta solves it

  • Unlimited sites on Business plan — one business, many stores; each store has its own geofence, QR code, and default shift times.
  • Per-store attendance comparison — compare lateness, overtime and absence across every store from the owner dashboard.
  • Scheduled shifts with expected times — rota the week ahead; lateness is measured against the schedule automatically.
  • Payroll runs and salary slips — batch-calculate pay per store, generate Recibo de Vencimento slips, and export SEPA files for the bank.

Head office sees every store's attendance in real time, and payroll runs from verified data instead of store-manager emails.

Healthcare & Care

Attendance compliance for healthcare staffing

Nurses and carers work across care homes, clinics, and home-visit routes. Overtime caps are a safety issue, not just a cost issue — and regulators expect accurate, auditable records of who cared for whom, when.

How Chegatta solves it

  • Overtime control per site — excessive-overtime flags are configured per location, protecting both residents and staff from unsafe consecutive hours.
  • Auditable time corrections — every manual adjustment to a shift time is logged with who made it and when; the worker is notified automatically.
  • Compliance monitoring by country — EU Working Time Directive rules applied per site; compliance issues surface before they become reportable incidents.
  • Verified attendance records — GPS-verified clock-ins at care homes provide the audit trail regulators and families expect.

Regulator-ready attendance records with overtime safely controlled across every facility.
