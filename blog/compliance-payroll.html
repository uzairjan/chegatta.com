Compliance & Regulations: Chegatta's Multi-Country Payroll Compliance

August 7, 2026 · Chegatta Team · 9 min read

How Chegatta automates compliance across US states and EU countries, reducing legal risk and saving $1,300+/month for mid-size agencies.

Chegatta is a comprehensive attendance tracking platform designed to ensure compliance for business clients and independent businesses operating across multiple jurisdictions — whether that's workers placed at different US states, or employees clocking in across EU countries like Portugal, Spain, France, and Germany.

Why Compliance Matters for Business Clients

business clients operate in a complex regulatory environment where rules around overtime, break penalties, holiday pay, and tax withholding vary significantly even within a single country. For a 50-employee business client with employees across multiple states/countries, non-compliance can easily cost €50,000–€200,000+ annually in fines, back pay, and legal fees.

Regulation Type Risk Potential Cost
Overtime laws Misclassifying overtime (daily/weekly thresholds, double-time) $100–$1,000 per violation × number of employees
Break penalties Missing meal/rest break payments (state-specific) $50–$500 per missed break
Holiday pay Incorrect holiday pay calculations 1.5–2× normal pay per holiday × affected employees
Tax withholding Wrong state/local tax deductions for remote workers Penalties + back taxes + interest
Record-keeping Inadequate audit trails for labor inspections $1,000–$10,000 per infraction

How Chegatta Ensures Compliance

1. Automatic Overtime Calculation by Jurisdiction

Chegatta applies each client's shift rules automatically based on employee location. When you add a work site, you specify the worksite country/state, and Chegatta knows the correct overtime thresholds, double-time rules, and meal/rest break penalties.

United States — State-by-State Overtime:

  • California: Daily overtime after 8 hours, double-time after 12 hours, 7th consecutive day overtime, meal/rest break penalties
  • New York: After 40 hours/week, double-time on holidays, meal break for shifts ≥6 hours
  • Texas: After 40 hours/week only (no daily overtime)
  • Florida: After 40 hours/week only (no daily overtime)
  • Illinois: After 8 hours/day or 40 hours/week, meal break for shifts ≥12 hours
  • Washington: Daily overtime after 40 hours/week, meal/rest break penalties
  • Massachusetts: After 8 hours/day, 20 minutes for shifts ≥6 hours, 30 minutes for shifts ≥8 hours

European Union — Country-by-Country:

  • Portugal: Maximum 40 hours/week, overtime premium ≥150%, 2-day rest period per 7-day period
  • Spain: Maximum 40 hours/week, overtime premium ≥125%, 12 hours rest between shifts
  • France: Maximum 35 hours/week (sector-dependent), overtime premium ≥125%, 11 hours rest between shifts
  • Germany: Maximum 48 hours/week (averaged over 4 months), overtime premium ≥150%, 24 hours rest per week

2. Break & Rest Period Enforcement

Chegatta detects and tracks all mandatory break periods automatically. When an employee clocks in, the system checks for required breaks based on jurisdiction rules and flags any violations before payroll is processed.

  • Minimum break duration (e.g., 30 minutes for every 4.5 hours worked)
  • Maximum consecutive work hours (e.g., no more than 6 hours without a break)
  • Minimum rest between shifts (e.g., 11 hours between shifts in EU, 10 hours in many US states)
  • Daily/weekly maximum working hours (jurisdiction-specific)

3. Holiday Pay & Premium Calculations

Chegatta maintains a database of statutory holidays and premium pay rates by country/state. When an employee works on a holiday, they receive automatic premium pay — calculated correctly based on their jurisdiction.

United States Federal Holidays:

New Year's Day, MLK Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day

European Union Holidays:

  • Portugal: 12–14 statutory holidays (varies by region), holiday pay at normal rate
  • Spain: 14 statutory holidays (including 2 local holidays), holiday pay at 100% or 150% depending on region
  • France: 11 statutory holidays, holiday pay at 100% + additional 10% for worked holidays
  • Germany: 9–13 statutory holidays (varies by state), holiday pay at 100% + Sunday premium 125–150%

4. Tax Withholding for Remote Workers

For agencies with employees working remotely in different states/countries, Chegatta tracks the worksite location for each employee and ensures correct tax withholding calculations. This prevents the costly confusion of multi-state tax registration and filing.

5. Audit-Ready Records

Every action in Chegatta creates an immutable audit trail. Whether it's a clock-in, a break period, an overtime calculation, or a payroll export — every action is timestamped, attributed, and stored with proper retention periods.

Action Stored Data Retention
Clock-in/clock-out GPS position, timestamp, device fingerprint 3 years (per EU labor law)
Break starts/ends Timestamp, duration 3 years
Overtime calculated Hours, rate, jurisdiction 4 years (per FLSA)
Holiday pay applied Date, rate, premium amount 4 years
Overtime thresholds applied Jurisdiction, rules used Permanent
Payroll export SEPA XML, payment details 7 years (per tax law)
