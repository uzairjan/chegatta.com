QR Code

QR code employee clock-in system

A QR code employee clock-in system lets workers scan a code at the client site to clock in, while GPS verification proves they're actually there. Chegatta combines QR scanning with GPS geofencing for double verification, tracks breaks and overtime automatically, and needs no app installation.

Why You Need It

QR codes add an extra layer of verification

QR codes at client sites prove workers are physically present, not just claiming they showed up. Combined with GPS verification, you get double proof of attendance. QR codes are also fast — workers scan and clock in in seconds.

QR code scanning

Generate a unique QR code for each client worksite. Workers scan the code with their phone camera to clock in. Fast, simple, and verifiable.

GPS + QR double verification

When a worker scans the QR code, Chegatta also captures their GPS position. Both must match the worksite, so you know they're really there.

No app installation

Workers use their phone's built-in camera to scan QR codes. No app to download, no account to create, no friction for temporary workers.

Dynamic QR codes

QR codes can be updated remotely if a worksite changes. No need to reprint physical codes when locations shift.

Automatic break tracking

Workers scan QR codes to start and end breaks. The system tracks break duration automatically against each client's rules.

Audit trail

Every QR scan is logged with timestamp, GPS coordinates, and worker ID. Perfect for compliance audits and dispute resolution.
