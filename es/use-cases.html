Security Staffing

Fichaje para agencias de seguridad

Guards work alone at client sites, often overnight. Without verification, you're paying for hours nobody can confirm — and a single falsified shift at one site can burn a client relationship worth months of revenue.

Cómo resuelve Chegatta

  • Geofenced clock-in at every client site — each guard posts' coordinates are checked against the site's GPS fence (default 200 m radius) at clock-in and clock-out.
  • Device fingerprinting — SHA-256 device identity catches a guard clocking in a colleague who hasn't arrived yet.
  • Late-start and early-leave flags — shifts that start late or end early against the scheduled rota appear on the attention list in real time.
  • Client visibility — give each client their own login so they see their site's coverage without emailing you for reports.
  • Offline resilience — basements and car parks with no signal still capture clock-ins; they sync when the phone reconnects.

Verified coverage evidence for every client site, disputes settled with data, and payroll built from confirmed hours instead of phone-call trust.

Hostelería y Eventos

Fichaje de personal temporal para hostelería y eventos

Camareros, cocineros y staff de eventos rotan entre centros — a veces varios en una semana. Las hojas de firma en papel se pierden, los descansos no se registran, y reconciliar quién trabajó dónde al final del mes consume días enteros.

Cómo resuelve Chegatta

  • Un trabajador, varios centros — asigna la misma persona a múltiples centros con un historial inmutable, para saber siempre quién trabajó dónde y cuándo.
  • Captura automática de descansos y horas extra — pausa/reanudar desde el móvil; horas extra calculadas por reglas del centro sin cálculos manuales.
  • Tratamiento de fichaje tardío — cuando alguien fichas después del inicio esperado más período de gracia, los gestores son notificados inmediatamente con una nota opcional del trabajador.
  • Comparación de asistencia por centro — identifica qué eventos consistentemente empiezan tarde o tienen poco personal desde un solo panel.

La reconciliación cae de días a minutos, y los clientes reciben horas exactas y detalladas para cada evento.

Preguntas frecuentes

Each work site has its own GPS geofence (coordinates plus a radius, default 200 m). Employees clock in from their phone browser, and Chegatta captures their coordinates and verifies they are inside the correct site's geofence. Clock-ins outside the fence are flagged for review — never blocked — so shifts always get recorded.

Yes. Chegatta is a web app — employees open a URL in their phone browser, no app install required. It is also an installable PWA that works offline: clock-ins taken without connection are queued and replayed automatically via background sync.

Chegatta fingerprints every device from HTTP headers (SHA-256) and compares it against the employee's trusted devices. Shared devices, new devices and frequent device changes are flagged as anomalies for review. Optional per-business settings let you require device approval and cap devices per employee.

Missing clock-outs are automatically flagged as anomalies on the dashboard's attention list. Admins can then correct the shift's clock-out time; every correction is written to an audit log and the employee is notified.
