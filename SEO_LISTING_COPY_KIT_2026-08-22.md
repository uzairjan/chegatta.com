## 4. Long description (~1200 chars — G2/Capterra "product overview")

> Chegatta is an attendance and time-tracking platform built for the way staffing agencies actually work: many workers, many client companies, many job sites.
>
> **Honest clock-ins.** Workers punch in from Android or iOS apps with GPS geofencing that only allows clock-in on-site, or by scanning a QR code at the workplace. A shared-tablet Kiosk mode covers teams whose employees don't carry smartphones — badge cards and PINs included.
>
> **Buddy punching ends here.** Every clock-in captures a server-side device fingerprint plus IP address. Shared-device anomalies are detected automatically, and trusted devices can be managed per employee.
>
> **From timesheet to payroll, automatically.** Attendance flows into salary calculations, automatic payslip generation (Recibo de Vencimento) and SEPA-ready payroll exports. Compliance rules encode the EU Working Time Directive and flag violations before they become fines.
>
> **Multi-tenant by design.** One agency dashboard manages multiple client companies, sites, contracts and workers, with role-based access for owners, agency admins, company managers and employees.
>
> **GDPR-first.** Privacy consent tracking, 90-day retention on IP/user-agent data, daily anonymization jobs, and full employee data rights.
>
> Start free (no card needed) or trial every feature for 14 days.
