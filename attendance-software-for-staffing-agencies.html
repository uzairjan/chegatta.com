Attendance Software

Attendance software for staffing agencies

Attendance software for staffing agencies tracks temporary employees placed with multiple client companies from one dashboard. Chegatta verifies every clock-in with GPS geofencing, tracks breaks and overtime automatically, and uses AI to flag attendance problems before they reach your clients.

Why You Need It

Spreadsheets and time clocks break when your people work everywhere

A staffing agency doesn't track one team at one office. It tracks hundreds of temporary workers across many client sites, shifts, and schedules. Generic tools can't verify who actually showed up, where, or for how long.

Employees placed everywhere

Your workers rotate between clients, sites, and shifts. You need one system that knows where each person is assigned today — not a pile of site-specific spreadsheets.

Verified, not just reported

Client companies pay for hours actually worked. Geofenced clock-in proves a worker was on site, which protects your billing and your reputation with clients.

Payroll-ready hours

Breaks and overtime are calculated automatically against each client's rules. No manual timesheet re-keying, no late-night arithmetic before payroll runs.

Many clients, one view

Compare attendance, lateness, and overtime across every client company from a single dashboard, so problem sites are visible before your client complains.

Problems flagged early

AI attention scores surface recurring lateness, missing clock-outs, and off-site clock-ins so you can fix issues before they escalate into client complaints.

Works in your workers' language

Employees clock in in English, Spanish, or Portuguese. No training manuals needed — a temporary worker from any market can clock in immediately.

How It Works

From signup to verified hours in one afternoon

1

Track every site in real time
