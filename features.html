Features

Everything you need to manage attendance

From GPS-verified clock-in to full payroll calculations, multi-country compliance, and native mobile apps — Chegatta gives you complete control over your workforce. Works for business clients AND independent businesses (restaurants, hotels, clinics, retail).

GPS-Verified Clock-In

Geolocation clock in/out with company geofencing. Ensure employees are at the right location when they mark attendance.

Break & Overtime Tracking

Automatic break detection and overtime calculation. Track worked hours, break time, and overtime down to the second.

Buddy Punching Prevention

Device fingerprinting (SHA-256) detects shared devices, new devices, and frequent device changes. Alerts you to potential time theft in real time.

Clients & Sites

Manage multiple work sites and work locations under one business. Assign employees to different sites, track rotations, and maintain full audit trails.

Full Payroll Calculations

Automatic payroll with overtime rules for US states (CA, NY, TX, FL, IL, WA, MA) and EU countries (PT, ES, FR). Includes daily OT, double time, meal/rest penalties, and holiday pay. Generate payslips (Recibo de Vencimento) and SEPA XML files for direct bank transfers.

Salary Runs & SEPA Exports

Build, approve, and process payroll runs with full salary calculations. Export pain.001 SEPA XML files for direct bank transfers — no manual data entry needed.

Contract Management

Track employee and service contracts with automatic expiry warnings. Stay on top of renewals and terminations with scheduled notifications.

Multi-Country Compliance

One business client, multiple sites. Each site gets its own labor law engine: EU Working Time Directive, US federal + 7 state rules. Holiday calendars included.

Native iOS & Android Apps

Full-featured native apps with offline clock-in, push notifications, device management, privacy consent, and geofencing. Feature parity with web.

Device Trust Management

Auto-trust first device, approve new devices, revoke access, set device limits (max 4). Employees manage their own devices; admins approve.

AI-Powered Insights

Detect anomalies, predict attendance patterns, and get attention scores for at-risk employees before problems escalate.

Web Push Notifications

Real-time browser push alerts for missed clock-outs, anomalies, and important attendance events. Never miss a thing.

Multi-Language Support

Full internationalization in English, Spanish (ES/MX), Portuguese (PT/BR), and French. Built for global business clients with diverse workforces.

Analytics

Insights that matter, not just data

Our analytics engine goes beyond basic reporting. It understands patterns, detects anomalies, and tells you where to focus your attention.

Anomaly Detection

Automatically flags off-site clock-ins, missing clock-outs, late arrivals, and excessive overtime.

Attention Scores

Weighted scoring system identifies at-risk employees so you can intervene before issues escalate.

Pattern Forecasting

Predicts expected attendance based on historical patterns and detects sudden breaks in routine.

Company Comparison

Compare attendance metrics across all your work sites in one view.
