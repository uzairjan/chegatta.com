This document explains how Chegatta helps business clients and independent businesses maintain payroll compliance across multiple countries (US states and EU countries), reducing legal risk and avoiding costly fines. ## Why Compliance Matters for Staffing Agencies

### The Compliance Challenge

Business clients operate in a complex regulatory environment:

| Regulation Type | Risk | Potential Cost |
|----------------|------|----------------|
| **Overtime laws** | Misclassifying overtime (daily/weekly thresholds, double-time) | $100–$1,000 per violation × number of employees |
| **Break penalties** | Missing meal/rest break payments (state-specific) | $50–$500 per missed break |
| **Holiday pay** | Incorrect holiday pay calculations | 1.5–2× normal pay per holiday × affected employees |
| **Tax withholding** | Wrong state/local tax deductions for remote workers | Penalties + back taxes + interest |
| **Record-keeping** | Inadequate audit trails for labor inspections | $1,000–$10,000 per infraction |

For a 50-employee business client with employees across multiple states/countries, non-compliance can easily cost $50,000–$200,000+ annually in fines, back pay, and legal fees.

## How Chegatta Ensures Compliance

### 1. Automatic Overtime Calculation by Jurisdiction

Chegatta applies each client's shift rules automatically based on employee location:

**United States — State-by-State Overtime:**
- **California:** Daily overtime after 8 hours, double-time after 12 hours, 7th consecutive day overtime, meal/rest break penalties
- **New York:** After 40 hours/week, double-time on holidays, meal break for shifts ≥6 hours
- **Texas:** After 40 hours/week only (no daily overtime)
- **Florida:** After 40 hours/week only (no daily overtime)
- **Illinois:** After 8 hours/day or 40 hours/week, meal break for shifts ≥12 hours
- **Washington:** Daily overtime after 40 hours/week, meal/rest break penalties
- **Massachusetts:** After 8 hours/day, 20 minutes for shifts ≥6 hours, 30 minutes for shifts ≥8 hours

**European Union — Country-by-Country:**
- **Portugal:** Maximum 40 hours/week, overtime premium ≥150%, 2-day rest period per 7-day period
- **Spain:** Maximum 40 hours/week, overtime premium ≥125%, 12 hours rest between shifts
- **France:** Maximum 35 hours/week (sector-dependent), overtime premium ≥125%, 11 hours rest between shifts
- **Germany:** Maximum 48 hours/week (averaged over 4 months), overtime premium ≥150%, 24 hours rest per week

**How It Works:**
When you add a client company, you specify the worksite country/state. Chegatta automatically:
- Applies the correct overtime thresholds
- Calculates double-time where required
- Applies meal/rest break penalties
- Generates compliant payslips (Recibo de Vencimento) with all mandatory fields
- Tracks overtime balances per employee per jurisdiction

### 2. Break & Rest Period Enforcement

Chegatta detects and tracks:

- **Minimum break duration** (e.g., 30 minutes for every 4.5 hours worked)
- **Maximum consecutive work hours** (e.g., no more than 6 hours without a break)
- **Minimum rest between shifts** (e.g., 11 hours between shifts in EU, 10 hours in many US states)
- **Daily/weekly maximum working hours** (jurisdiction-specific)

**Compliance Output:**
- Payslips show break time taken and any penalties applied
- Overtime reports include jurisdiction-specific calculations
- Audit trails record every break and rest period
- Non-compliant patterns are flagged via AI attention scoring

### 3. Holiday Pay & Premium Calculations

Chegatta maintains a database of statutory holidays and premium pay rates by country/state:

**United States:**
- Federal holidays: New Year's Day, MLK Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day
- State-specific holidays (varies by state)
- Premium pay: 1.5× for overtime, 2× for holidays in some jurisdictions

**European Union:**
- **Portugal:** 12–14 statutory holidays (varies by region), holiday pay at normal rate
- **Spain:** 14 statutory holidays (including 2 local holidays), holiday pay at 100% or 150% depending on region
- **France:** 11 statutory holidays, holiday pay at 100% + additional 10% for worked holidays
- **Germany:** 9–13 statutory holidays (varies by state), holiday pay at 100% + Sunday premium 125–150%

**How It Works:**
- When configuring a client, select the country/state
- Chegatta's system knows the statutory holidays and premium rates
- Employees working on holidays get automatic premium pay
- Payslips clearly show holiday pay as a separate line item
- Year-end reports include holiday utilization per employee

### 4. Tax Withholding for Remote Workers

For agencies with employees working in different states/countries:

**United States:**
- Chegatta tracks the worksite state for each employee
- Automatic tax withholding calculations based on employee's worksite state
- Generates state-specific tax forms (where applicable)
- Audit trail for multi-state tax compliance

**European Union:**
- Employees taxed per their country of residence
- Chegatta tracks residence country for each employee
- Payroll reports formatted for each country's tax authority
- Compliance with EU cross-border telework regulations
