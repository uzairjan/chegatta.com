# Chegatta — Staffing Agency Outreach & Lead-Gen Plan (PT + ES)

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: Saved plan (validated sources)

## Purpose
Build legitimate B2B outreach to temporary-employment / staffing agencies in **Portugal** and **Spain** for selling Chegatta (time tracking / HR software), without mass-scraping personal emails/phones (which violates GDPR / AVG / LOPDGDD).

## Approach (4 tracks — all user-approved)
1. Public directory lead list (company-level data only)
2. Targeted per-site research (CRM-ready tracker)
3. GDPR-compliant outreach copy
4. Market/positioning research

## Track 2 — Targeted per-site research (CRM tracker)
- For each target firm, visit its own public website (from directory/registry) and record only the **publicly-stated business contact** (general `info@...`, contact form, office phone on their Contact page). Do not harvest employee personal emails/phones.
- Maintain a CRM-ready tracker (CSV/Google Sheet): firm, city, website, business email, office phone, company size, sector, notes, contact status.
- Prefer the **decision-maker route**: general business contact → request intro to the person who owns HR/ops software decisions, rather than scraping named individuals.

## Track 3 — GDPR-compliant outreach copy
- Lawful basis: legitimate interest (B2B business contact) — document the balancing test; keep data minimal and fresh.
- Cold email: clear identity, one clear CTA, honest subject, no deceptive framing.
- **Mandatory**: visible unsubscribe, working opt-out, data-retention window, privacy notice pointer. Honors both AVG (PT) and LOPDGDD + ePrivacy (ES).
- Volume: send low-volume, personalized campaigns; warm up domain; avoid spam-trap patterns. Track deliverability (SPF/DKIM/DMARC).
- Templates: PT and ES local-language drafts (Portuguese + Spanish), not machine-glitch.

## Track 4 — Market / positioning research
- Use association membership as a signal of seriousness (licensed + association = quality). Chegatta differentiators to lead with: staffing-agency multi-client/multi-tenant model, EU compliance (SEPA, Recibo de Vencimento, PT/ES languages), full HR loop (leave, contracts, payroll) vs. Jibble which avoids payroll.
- Reaching agencies: associations (APESPE RH, ASEMPLEO, AGETT), industry events, SEO for "software ETT" / "software trabalho temporário", content for staffing ops pain points.

## Compliance notes
- Do **not** bulk-scrape personal emails/phones from the web or social.
- Company-level registry data (firm name, legal form, NIPC/CIF, registered address) is firmographic and far lower-risk.
- When collecting any personal data, apply GDPR: purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation, and a lawful basis.
