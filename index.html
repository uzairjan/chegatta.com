Features

Everything you need to manage attendance

From GPS-verified clock-in to full payroll calculations, multi-country compliance, and native mobile apps — Chegatta gives you complete control over your workforce.

GPS-Verified Clock-In

Geolocation clock in/out with company geofencing. Ensure employees are at the right location when they mark attendance.

Break & Overtime Tracking

Automatic break detection and overtime calculation. Track worked hours, break time, and overtime down to the second.

Buddy Punching Prevention

Device fingerprinting (SHA-256) detects shared devices, new devices, and frequent device changes. Alerts you to potential time theft in real time.

Multi-Company Assignment

Assign employees to multiple work sites. Track assignments, manage rotations, and maintain full audit trails.

Full Payroll Calculations

Automatic payroll with overtime rules for US states (CA, NY, TX, FL, IL, WA, MA) and EU countries (PT, ES, FR). Includes daily OT, double time, meal/rest penalties, and holiday pay.

Multi-Country Compliance

One business client, multiple sites. Each site gets its own labor law engine: EU Working Time Directive, US federal + 7 state rules. Holiday calendars included.

Native iOS & Android Apps

Full-featured native apps with offline clock-in, push notifications, device management, privacy consent, and geofencing. Feature parity with web.

Device Trust Management

Auto-trust first device, approve new devices, revoke access, set device limits (max 4). Employees manage their own devices; admins approve.

AI-Powered Insights

Detect anomalies, predict attendance patterns, and get attention scores for at-risk employees before problems escalate.

Web Push Notifications

Real-time browser push alerts for missed clock-outs, anomalies, and important attendance events. Never miss a thing.

Multi-Language Support

Full internationalization in English, Spanish (ES/MX), Portuguese (PT/BR), and French. Built for global business clients with diverse workforces.

Chegatta uses device fingerprinting (SHA-256 hash of device characteristics) plus GPS verification. Every clock-in is tied to a specific trusted device. The system detects shared devices, new devices, multiple devices in one day, and frequent device changes — alerting you to potential buddy punching attempts in real time.

Yes! Chegatta calculates payroll with full overtime rules for your jurisdiction. For US states (CA, NY, TX, FL, IL, WA, MA) it handles daily overtime, double time (CA), 7th day overtime (CA), meal/rest break penalties, spread of hours (NY), split shift premiums, and reporting time pay. For EU countries (PT, ES, FR) it handles EU Working Time Directive rules. All calculations are exportable for payroll processing.

Each company in your business can be in a different country/state with its own labor laws. During signup you select the country (and US state). Chegatta currently supports English, Spanish (Spain & Mexico), Portuguese (Portugal & Brazil), and French. The interface automatically detects the user's browser language. Additional languages can be added through our translation system.
