Features

Everything you need to manage attendance

From geolocation clock-in to AI-powered insights, Chegatta gives you complete control over your workforce.

Geolocation Clock-In

GPS-verified clock in/out with company geofencing. Ensure employees are at the right location when they mark attendance.

Break & Overtime Tracking

Automatic break detection and overtime calculation. Track worked hours, break time, and overtime down to the second.

AI-Powered Insights

Detect anomalies, predict attendance patterns, and get attention scores for at-risk employees before problems escalate.

Multi-Company Assignment

Assign employees to multiple client companies. Track assignments, manage rotations, and maintain full audit trails.

Web Push Notifications

Real-time browser push alerts for missed clock-outs, anomalies, and important attendance events. Never miss a thing.

Multi-Language Support

Full internationalization in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. Built for European staffing agencies with diverse workforces.

Analytics

Insights that matter, not just data

Our analytics engine goes beyond basic reporting. It understands patterns, detects anomalies, and tells you where to focus your attention.

Anomaly Detection

Automatically flags off-site clock-ins, missing clock-outs, late arrivals, and excessive overtime.

Attention Scores

Weighted scoring system identifies at-risk employees so you can intervene before issues escalate.

Pattern Forecasting

Predicts expected attendance based on historical patterns and detects sudden breaks in routine.

Company Comparison

Compare attendance metrics across all your client companies in one view.
