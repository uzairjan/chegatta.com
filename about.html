About Us

Attendance tracking that tells the truth

Chegatta builds software for organizations where people work across many sites — staffing agencies, field teams, and multi-company operations that can't trust a spreadsheet or a time clock.

Our Story

Why we built Chegatta

1

The problem

Staffing agencies manage hundreds of workers across dozens of client sites. Hours were reported, not verified. Breaks and overtime were reconstructed by hand. Every month ended with someone re-keying timesheets into payroll.

2

The idea

Attendance data should be collected automatically, verified by location, and turned into insights — not spreadsheets. So we built a system around geofenced clock-in, automatic break and overtime tracking, and AI that flags problems early.

3

Today

Chegatta helps agencies across Europe track attendance in English, Spanish, and Portuguese — with no app to install, no hardware, and no per-client complexity.

What We Believe

Our principles

Verification over trust

People and systems make mistakes. Geolocation and automatic rules catch them before they cost money or cause disputes.

Simple for the person clocking in

The person on the ground should just tap to clock in. No training, no installation, no friction — regardless of their language.

Privacy by design

Cookieless analytics, role-based access, and data stored securely. We collect only what is needed to run attendance accurately.
